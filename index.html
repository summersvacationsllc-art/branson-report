What's biting: Largemouth and spotted bass are schooled deep on main-lake structure. Top techniques — deep crankbaits (chartreuse/blue) in 15–25 ft, drop-shot on main-lake points, flutter spoons on deep timber. Early morning topwater on shaded banks. Bluegill are swarming shallows — perfect for kids with worms under a bobber.

Hot areas: Upper Kings River arm (active shad), James River arm (stained water, channel-swing points), main-lake points on the lower end (drop-shot zone).

What's biting: Rainbow trout are crushing pink power worms under a float during generation (starts ~noon). The Provo rig (double scud or mega worm + egg fly) is deadly in the upper lake. Trophy zone (upper 1.5 miles below dam) holds quality browns on #14 gray scud patterns. Recent catches include a 23.5-inch, 5.5 lb rainbow!

Best times: Early morning before generation kicks in, then drift-fishing during peak generation (2–8 PM). Generators start ~noon, ramp to 200 MW by 2 PM. The evening bite is excellent.

Gear tip: 2–4 lb test line. Bring a jacket — even on a 90°F day, the boat ride on Taneycomo is chilly (46°F water).
