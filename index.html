🎣 Fishing Report

"Start early — the heat is real. On Table Rock, be on the water by 6 AM for topwater before the sun cooks the surface."

Table Rock Lake ★★★★☆

Surface temp: 78°F · Elevation: 917.0 ft (summer pool)

Topwater bass at dawn on main lake points — Whopper Ploppers, walking baits, buzzbaits. As sun climbs, go deep: deep crankbaits, flutter spoons, creature baits in 15–25 ft.

Bluegill: ★★★★★ July peak! Swarming shallows — perfect for kids with crickets or worms on light tackle.

Walleye: ★★★★☆ Good — 15–30 ft on deep points and channel swings.

Crappie: ★★★☆☆ Fair — 15–25 ft around brush piles and docks.

Lake Taneycomo ★★★★☆

Water temp: 48–52°F (released from deep Table Rock) · Clarity: Excellent

Morning low-water window is prime. Micro jigs under a float for 40+ fish days. The Branson Landing area is the current hot spot — schools of rainbows eager to eat.

Fly fishing: Small midges, scuds, micro jigs in olive/gray/brown. 2 lb test or 6x tippet in the clear water.

Recent trophy: 24" / 6.12 lb rainbow caught on Taneycomo. As Phil Lilley says: "It's very possible for even the most inexperienced angler to catch a truly 'fish of a lifetime' trophy on Lake Taneycomo this summer."

Generation pattern: Low flow (~20 cfs) midnight–6 AM, ramping to ~12,800 cfs by midday. Morning is your best window. Check generation schedules before heading out.
