Fishing Report

Bite of the Day

Morning no-generation windows on Lake Taneycomo = prime wading conditions. Target rainbows on pink worms under a float below Fall Creek. On Table Rock, throw topwater (Whopper Plopper) for bass before the heat sets in, then switch to deep crankbaits and flutter spoons over main lake points at 10–25 ft.

🏞️ Table Rock Lake

Water 78.6°F · Clear main lake · Lake level 916.92 ft

Largemouth & Spotted Bass — Excellent. Summer pattern set. Fish have moved to offshore structure at 10–25 ft. Topwater bite still on early. Deep crankbaits, flutter spoons, creature baits, and jigs are producing.

Walleye — Good. Near dam, upper Kings River, James River, and White River arms. Troll near deep points and channel swings.

Bluegill — Season Peak. Quality fish at 7–9" in gravel areas at 10–20 ft.

Catfish — Good. Channel cats in river arms; flatheads in clearer main lake sections near woody structure.

🎣 Lake Taneycomo

Water 50°F · Very clear · No generation until ~1–2 PM

Rainbow Trout — Good to Very Good. Fishing has improved over the last couple of weeks. The morning no-generation window means excellent wading conditions below Fall Creek. Pink worm under a float is the #1 summer bait. Branson Landing area has stocked rainbows schooled up — a guy throwing a spoon from the pier near Turkey Creek was catching on almost every cast.

Hot spots: Below Fall Creek (pink worm, jig, mega worm under float) · Trophy area (scuds, mega worm + egg, zebra midges) · Fall Creek to Cooper Creek (night crawlers for trophy trout)

Tip: Use 2-lb test line — the water is very clear. No felt-soled wading boots allowed in Missouri.

📝 Summary: Morning is the magic window on both lakes before the July heat builds and before Taneycomo's afternoon generation surge. Stay hydrated — heat index forecast 105° today.
