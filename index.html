🎣 Fishing Report
Table Rock Lake & Lake Taneycomo

Start early — the heat index hits 104°F today. On the water by 6 AM for the best action.

Table Rock Lake

★★★★☆ Good
  • Surface temp: 78°F · Water clear · Lake at summer pool (917.0 ft)
  • Best bet: Topwater bass at dawn on main lake points — Whopper Ploppers and walking baits before 8 AM
  • Midday: Deep crankbaits, flutter spoons in 15–25 ft
  • Bluegill (Excellent): Swarming shallows in 5–20 ft — perfect for kids with crickets or worms!
  • Walleye (Good): Deep points and channel swings in 15–30 ft

Lake Taneycomo

★★★★☆ Good
  • Water temp: 48–52°F (released from deep Table Rock) · Crystal clear
  • Best bet: Low-water morning window before 7 AM — micro jigs under a float for 40+ fish days
  • Hot spot: Branson Landing area — rainbows on almost every cast near Scotty's Dock
  • Fly fishing: Small midges, scuds, micro jigs in olive, gray, brown. 2 lb test or 6x tippet in clear water.
  • Trophy note: A 24" / 6.12 lb rainbow caught recently. "Even the most inexperienced angler can catch a fish of a lifetime."
