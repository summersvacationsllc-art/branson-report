🎣 Fishing Report

🟢 Bite of the Day

Taneycomo trout are biting strong on pink worms under floats below Fall Creek — no generation this morning means prime wading conditions. Table Rock bass are transitioning to summer patterns with topwater heating up at dawn.

🌊 Table Rock Lake

MetricCondition
Lake Elevation916.86 ft — just below summer pool
Water Temp77–80°F main lake; 77.2°F at Kimberling City
ClarityClear main lake; stained in river arms
Generation0 MW until ~1 PM, then 100–200 MW afternoon

Bass

Fish suspending 12–30 ft over deeper water. Topwater at dawn/dusk with Whopper Ploppers and Spooks in main lake coves. Deep crankbaits (Strike King 6XD/8XD) on gravel points 15–25 ft. Drop shot with plum/Morning Dawn on points 20–30 ft. Good areas: Long Creek, Indian Point, Aunts Creek.

Walleye — Excellent!

Guides report 30 to 50 fish per trip. Crawler harnesses and deep-diving crankbaits on main lake flats and gravel points. Night fishing near creek mouths is productive.

Crappie

Fair. Post-spawn fish in 15–25 ft. Live minnows or Bobby Garland Swimming Minnows in standing timber and around dock cables in the James River Arm.

🎣 Lake Taneycomo — Trout

MetricCondition
Generation Today0 MW until ~1 PM — excellent wading morning! Then 100–200 MW through evening
Water ClarityVery clear — use 2-lb line or 6x tippet
Water TempCold — 50–55°F near dam

Hot Flies & Baits

  • Pink worm under a float — Phil Lilley's top pick
  • Zebra midge (olive, black, red) in Trophy Area
  • Nightcrawlers drifted on bottom — guides reporting trophy trout
  • PowerBait (chartreuse, pink, orange) for stocker rainbows
  • Kastmaster spoons from Branson Landing pier

Best Areas

  • Trophy Area (below dam) — small flies, 2-lb test
  • Fall Creek to Cooper Creek — pink worm under float, nightcrawlers
  • Branson Landing — spoons from the pier, stocked rainbows
  • Eddies & slack water — drifting bait in afternoon flows

💡 Insider tip from Phil Lilley: "It's very possible for even the most inexperienced angler to catch a truly 'fish of a lifetime' trophy on Lake Taneycomo this summer." Get out early — no generation until 1 PM means calm, clear water. Drop down to 2-lb test if you're not getting bites on 4-lb.

🏎️ 76 Strip & Family Fun

🎡 Branson Ferris Wheel

15 stories tall with 16,000 LED lights — originally from Chicago's Navy Pier! Catch the music & light spectacular at 8, 9, and 10 PM nightly at Track 4. $12.49 kids, $15.49 adults.

🚜 Dig Town Adventure Park — BRAND NEW in 2026!

Branson's newest attraction! A construction-themed adventure park where kids can dig, drive heavy equipment, and operate real machines. On the 76 Strip — nothing else like it in Branson.

🏎️ The Track Family Fun Parks

Three locations on the Strip with go-karts for every age — from the wooden Wild Woody (Track 3, original since 1981) to the four-story Heavy Metal steel track (Track 4) to the massive LumberJack (Track 5) with laser tag, glow golf, bumper boats, and batting cages. Unlimited Summer Pass available through Sept 6 — ride everything at all 3 parks!

🏔️ Copperhead Mountain Coaster

Branson's longest downhill mountain coaster at Shepherd of the Hills Adventure Park. 3,400 feet of loops, drops, and curves through the Ozark Mountains. $17.95 driver, open until 9 PM (night rides!). Also home to USA Today's #1 Aerial Adventure Park in America.

🌀 Fritz's Adventure

80,000+ sq ft of climate-controlled indoor adventure. Climb a real water tower, crawl through a suspended airplane, race down a 32-ft spiral slide, explore 330+ ft of underground tunnels. All-day pass $36.99. Perfect for hot or rainy afternoons!

⚡ Xtreme Racing Center

North America's longest high-speed kart track — 3,500 ft of concrete raceway. $10 OFF a 3-race package this season. Drivers must be 60" tall.

🌊 White Water Water Park

Open daily 10 AM–7 PM through Sept 7. Extended hours this Friday July 10 — open until 10 PM! Combo tickets with Silver Dollar City available.

🧪 WonderWorks

Walk into an upside-down building! 48,000 sq ft with 100+ hands-on STEAM exhibits. Open 10 AM–9 PM, 365 days a year. Free parking.

🔮 World of Wizards Blacklight Mini-Golf

Enchanted indoor blacklight mini golf with wizards, elves, and mystical creatures. 3D glasses upgrade available. Consistently rated one of the best mini golf experiences in Branson.

🐠 Aquarium at the Boardwalk

10 zones including Jellyfish Infinity Room, Kelp Forest, Shipwreck Cove, and a 5D submarine ride. 7,200+ sea animals. $35.99 adult / $19.99 child online.

🚂 Branson Scenic Railway

1h 45m round trip through Ozark foothills in vintage 1950s dining cars. Departs from Downtown Branson Depot (206 E Main St).

🌊 Branson Landing Fountain Show — FREE

Choreographed fountain show on Lake Taneycomo with music and lights in the evening. Combine with dinner at the Landing for a perfect summer evening.

🌿 Free & Easy: The Ruth & Paul Henning Conservation Area (near SDC and the Strip) has 1,534 acres of free hiking with 3 trails from 0.5–2.5 miles. Great for a morning walk before the heat. Also check out the Farmers Markets — Branson Landing on Tuesdays 2:30–6:30 PM, and StateoftheOzarks in Hollister on Wednesdays 2–6 PM & Saturdays 10 AM–2 PM.
