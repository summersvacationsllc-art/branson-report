Fishing Report

🎯 Bite of the Day: Morning topwater bass on Table Rock is heating up — hit the points early with Whopper Ploppers before the sun gets high. On Taneycomo, the morning low-water window is your best bet; micro jigs in olive/gray under a float are producing 40+ fish days.

🐟 Table Rock Lake

★★★★☆ Good

Surface temp: 77–85°F
Clarity: Clear to slight stain
Elevation: 916.88 ft

Best bets: Topwater bass at dawn (Whopper Ploppers, buzzbaits). Midday: deep crankbaits, creature baits on points and humps in 15–25 ft. Bluegill are ★★★★★ excellent and swarming shallows — perfect for teaching kids to fish.

🐟 Lake Taneycomo

★★★★☆ Good

Water temp: ~48–52°F (brrr, that's Table Rock's deep release!)
Clarity: Excellent — clean presentations essential
Flow: Min ~20 cfs overnight, peak ~12,800 cfs midday

Best bets: Morning low-water window with micro jigs (olive, gray, brown) under small floats. During generation, drift seam edges and current breaks. A 24"/6.12 lb rainbow was caught recently — the big ones are there, just selective.

Quick Tips from the Guides

  • Early bird wins. On Table Rock, be on the water by 6 AM for explosive topwater action before the heat and boat traffic settle in.
  • Downsize midday. 85°F surface temps and no wind mean fish get picky — slow down, downsize your presentation.
  • Taneycomo low water is a gift. The overnight minimum flow (~20 cfs) gives waders and dock fishermen a prime window before generation ramps up around 6–10 AM.
  • Kids love bluegill. Table Rock's shallows are swarming with bluegill right now. Crickets or worms on light tackle = instant smiles.

Three locations with go-karts, bumper boats, mini golf, laser tag, and arcade. The Heavy Metal High Rise elevated track and the classic Wild Woody wooden track are favorites. $45 All-Day Pass available.

Copperhead Mountain Coaster (Branson's longest), ziplines, ropes course, Inspiration Tower. Voted #1 Aerial Adventure Park in America. Coaster runs 10 AM–9 PM daily.

🏢 WonderWorks Branson

The famous upside-down building! 48,000 sq ft of hands-on science fun — earthquake simulator, hurricane tunnel, laser tag, 4D motion ride. Open daily 10 AM–9 PM.

🧗 Fritz's Adventure

80,000+ sq ft of indoor climbing, ziplining, and sliding through a suspended airplane, a water tower, and underground tunnels. #worldsfirst indoor/outdoor zipline!

36 holes of outdoor mining-themed mini golf. Ride a mining train to the top! Adults $15, Kids $13. A classic Branson mini-golf experience.
