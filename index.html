🎣 Fishing Report

👍 Good

Table Rock Lake

Surface temp: 76–80°F · Elevation: 916.91 ft (near full pool)

What's biting: Deep crankbaits (chartreuse/blue) in 15–25 ft on channel-swing banks. Drop-shot on main-lake points is steady. Flutter spoon on deep timber for reaction strikes. Bluegill are swarming the shallows — perfect for kids with a bobber!

Best bet: Early morning topwater before the sun gets high, then deep crankbaits and drop-shot through the afternoon. Fireworks view from the lake tonight — bring the boat!

👍 Good–Excellent

Lake Taneycomo

Water temp: ~46°F below dam (cold year-round) · Clarity: Excellent

What's biting: Pink power worm under a float during generation (starts ~noon) — the hottest pattern this week! A 5.5 lb, 23.5" trophy rainbow was caught July 2. Provo rig (double scud + egg fly) in the trophy zone for fly anglers.

Generation today: 0 MW overnight → 40 MW noon → 200 MW peak 2–8 PM → off by 10 PM. Drift during generation is prime. Tip: Bring a jacket — the boat ride is chilly even on a 90° July day!

🍽️ Where to Eat

🥞 Billy Gail's — Home of the famous 14-inch "hubcap" pancake. A Branson breakfast institution with 3,300+ reviews and a 4.6★ rating. Opens 7 AM — go early or expect a wait!
🌮 Gettin' Basted — KCBS Grand Champion BBQ on the Strip. Wagyu brisket, championship burnt ends, gochujang maple-glazed wings. Open 7 AM–10 PM daily (yes, breakfast BBQ is a thing here).
🎵 Pickin' Porch Grill — Live bluegrass music Tues/Thurs/Sat 5–6:30 PM inside Branson Craft Mall. Charbroiled burgers, catfish, fried green tomatoes. 4.8★ with 1,486 reviews.
🎤 Mel's Hard Luck Diner — 1950s diner where the servers are trained singers (many from Branson's biggest shows). Wagyu MOINK burger, huge desserts, milkshakes. Closed Sunday.
🏌️ The Social Birdy — Branson's newest hot spot — #1 rated on TripAdvisor (4.9★). Fried chicken sandwiches, smashburgers, indoor golf simulators, pickleball courts. Think Topgolf meets Branson.
🐟 White River Fish House — Floating restaurant on Lake Taneycomo at Branson Landing. Fresh trout, catfish, Key Lime Pie. Perfect for pre-fireworks dinner with a lake view.
☕ Steamy Joe Cafe — Best Breakfast in Branson award winner. Ozark mountain views, Foxen Coffee, Blueberry Lemon Ricotta Pancakes. Open 7 AM–2 PM daily. Family-owned gem.
🥩 Level 2 Steakhouse — AAA Four-Diamond dining at the Hilton. 28-day dry-aged Hereford beef, Hawaiian seafood flown in twice weekly, sommelier-curated wine list. Branson's top date-night steakhouse.
🏡 Local Flavor Branson — Southern comfort food that "feels like Grandma's house." Chicken fried steak, fried green tomatoes, homemade desserts. 4.7★. Closed Monday.
🌾 Fall Creek Steak & Catfish House — Home of the "tossed rolls" — since 1991. Hand-tossed dinner rolls, sizzling ribeyes, fried catfish. Great value for families. Also serves breakfast!
🍗 Paula Deen's Family Kitchen — Family-style Southern dining at Branson Landing. Fried chicken, biscuits, mac & cheese — served on platters for the table. $24.99/person. Fountain-view patio seating.
🌳 Crazy Craig's Treehouse — A literal three-level treehouse bar in downtown Branson. Massive nachos, one-pound wings, tropical drinks. Branson's most unique dining atmosphere.
