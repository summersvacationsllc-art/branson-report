Fishing Report

Report from July 2 — conditions still valid through the holiday weekend.

Table Rock Lake

Level: 917.28 ft (+0.3 ft above pool)
Temp: 77.6°F
Clarity: Clear main lake, stained arms
🟢 Bass · Top Pick
  • Early AM: Topwater on main-lake bluff points — Cow Creek area and Kimberling City producing. PBJ shaky head deadly.
  • Late AM → PM: Move to 20–35 ft ledges with deep crankbaits, drop-shot (green pumpkin), shaky-head, Carolina rig.
  • Walleye: Schooled on deep rock 20–35 ft — crawler harnesses, nightcrawler rigs.
  • Crappie: Brush piles 18–25 ft. Minnows or small jigs chartreuse/white.
  • Bluegill: Great for kids — worms under a bobber near docks.

Guides reporting 30–50 fish days. Start by 5:30–6 AM before the heat peaks.

Lake Taneycomo

Temp: 46–52°F (cold tailwater)
Flow: Low AM, heavy generation PM
Clarity: Excellent — clean presentations
🟢 Trout · Morning Window
  • Fly fishing / wading (AM): Wade Trophy Run below dam. Gray scuds (#14–18), Ruby 2 midge, black copperhead midge. Provo Rig with double scud or mega worm + egg deadly.
  • Boat / drift (AM): Trout Magnet (salmon, 1/80 oz) under float at 4–6 ft. Big schools near MDC boat dock.
  • Hot colors: Red/pink San Juan worms, cerise San Juan worm, black copperhead midge, Ruby 2.
  • ⚠️ Heavy generation 3–9 PM (200 MW, ~13,000 cfs) — fish early, be off the water by noon or prepare for rising water.

Dress in layers! 94°F air vs. 46°F water. Felt-soled boots prohibited in MO.

Where to Eat

Branson Landing

White River Fish House

A floating barge restaurant perched right on Lake Taneycomo at Bass Pro Shops. World Famous Islamorada Fish Sandwich, Brown Sugar Glazed Salmon, Ozarks Trout Almondine. Floor-to-ceiling windows with fountain show views. Perfect pre-fireworks dinner spot.

Landing · Only in MO

Guy Fieri's Branson Kitchen + Bar

The only Guy Fieri restaurant in Missouri! Bold flavors, scratch-made burgers, Trash Can Nachos, bacon-wrapped meatloaf. Rock-and-roll energy with big portions and "zero regrets" comfort food. Open Sun–Thu 11-9, Fri–Sat 11-10.

Landing · Southern

Paula Deen's Family Kitchen

Endless family-style portions of Southern classics — fried chicken, chicken & dumplings, garlic cheddar biscuits. Every meal ends with a dessert of your choice. Also has a Paula Deen retail store with 3,000+ items.

BBQ · Championship

Gettin' Basted

Competition-level BBQ — brisket, burnt ends, Basted Nachos, St. Louis Cut Ribs. "Best BBQ in Missouri" contender. Now serving breakfast 7–11 AM daily too! The Judge's Plate ($68) gives you a little of everything.

BBQ · #1 in Branson

Big D's BBQ

Voted BEST BBQ in Branson every year since 2018. Texas-style brisket, Memphis-style ribs, smoked half chicken. Check their Facebook for July 4th off-menu specials. Family-owned with loyal local following.

Breakfast Icon

Billy Gail's

Legendary 14-inch plate-sized Sweet Creme Vanilla Pancakes — one pancake covers an entire plate! Voted best breakfast in Branson year after year. Open 7 AM daily. Extended hours until 9 PM this holiday weekend.

Unique · Singing Servers

Mel's Hard Luck Diner

A 1950s-style diner where the servers sing! Professional performers take your order and break into show tunes mid-meal. Classic diner fare — patty melts, chili burgers, Jimmy Crack Corn Chicken Chowder. One-of-a-kind Branson experience.

Table Rock · Italian

Cellar 417

A hidden gem on Table Rock Lake. All pasta made in-house — Cacio e Pepe, Beef Cheek Sugo. Stunning lake views, dog-friendly patio, wine shop on site. Recently featured in 417 Magazine. Perfect date-night spot.

Farm-to-Table

The Keeter Center at CofO

Student-run farm-to-table dining at College of the Ozarks. Vegetables from the campus garden, pork from campus-raised hogs, legendary house-made ice cream. Dinner reservations after 4 PM. Lunch walk-in 10:30 AM–4 PM.

Hollister · Gastro Pub

Downing Street Pour House

Just 5 minutes from the Landing in downtown Hollister. D-Street Nachos, Half Basted Wings, great beer selection. Laid-back historic building with a summer patio. Open Mon–Thu 11-9, Fri–Sat 11-10.

🥩 Weekend Specials

Fall Creek Steak & Catfish House — Smoked Prime Rib Friday & Saturday (July 3 & 4) from 4 PM until sold out.
Level 2 Steakhouse — Branson's premier fine dining. Personalized engraved steak knife with every steak. Chappellet wine dinner coming July 15.
The Social Birdy — Hand-crafted fried chicken sandwiches + mini golf and pickleball. Dinner AND activities!

76 Strip & Family Fun

Go-Karts · Classic

The Track — Wild Woody

Track 3 at 2505 W 76. Branson's iconic wooden high-rise go-kart track — 45 years strong! Go-karts, spinning coaster, pirate ship ride, and kiddie karts. The OG Track experience.

Go-Karts · Adrenaline

The Track — Heavy Metal High Rise

Track 4 at 3345 W 76. Elevated go-kart track, SkyCoaster (100 ft up!), bumper boats, and the Branson Ferris Wheel with 16,000 LEDs. Nightly music & light spectacular. Extended hours for July 4th.

Indoor Glow Golf

The Track — The LumberJack

Track 5 at 3525 W 76. LumberJack high-rise karts, indoor Glow Golf (blacklight mini golf), and laser tag. Perfect for beating the afternoon heat.

New for 2026

Xtreme Racing Center

North America's longest high-speed racing kart track — 3,500 feet! European-style racing karts. Brand-new track opened March 2026. $10 off 3-race package available. 60" height minimum.

#1 Aerial Adventure

Shepherd of the Hills Adventure Park

Voted #1 Aerial Adventure Park in America! Copperhead Mountain Coaster ($17.95 driver) — Branson's longest, fastest downhill coaster. Vigilante Extreme ZipRider from 230-ft Inspiration Tower. Ropes course, mini golf, and more. Sunset views from Inspiration Tower are spectacular.

Indoor · Rainy Day

Fritz's Adventure

80,000+ sq ft of creative climbing. Explore a suspended 1959 Beechcraft airplane, climb through a repurposed water tower, scale a 48-ft indoor brick wall. #WorldsFirst indoor/outdoor zipline course. 2026 Best of Southwest Missouri Awards winner.

Upside-Down Building

WonderWorks

One of the most photographed spots on the Strip — an upside-down building! 100+ hands-on S.T.E.A.M. exhibits including earthquake simulator, hurricane chamber, zero-gravity experience, and laser tag. Open 365 days, 10 AM–9 PM.

Indoor Fun

Fun Spot at Grand Country

Year-round indoor fun — Mountain Ridge Racer indoor go-karts, Black Light Mini-Golf (USA Today Top 10), laser tag, XD Dark Ride, mini-bowling. Three mini golf courses! Great for hot afternoons.

Mini Golf

Lost Treasure Golf

Two 18-hole mining-themed courses. Ride a mining car to the top and putt through caves and treasure tunnels. Open 9 AM–11 PM daily through Sept 7. Evening putt-putt under the lights!

Mountain Coaster

Runaway Mountain Coaster

The first mountain coaster in the Ozarks — 4,900 ft of user-controlled gravity-powered thrill ride through Ozark woods. Also has the longest gem mine in the Ozarks. Ages 3+ can ride as passengers.

🎆 July 4th Weekend Bonus

The Branson Ferris Wheel has special evening light shows all weekend. Shepherd of the Hills Inspiration Tower offers panoramic views of fireworks across Branson. All attractions running extended hours through the holiday weekend.
