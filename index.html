July 4th Fireworks & Events

America's 250th Birthday Weekend — Branson is going all out!

The biggest weekend of the summer. Six fireworks shows, military bands, free concerts, and patriotic celebrations all across Branson.

🔥 TONIGHT

Fireburst Fireworks over Table Rock Lake

A community tradition for 40+ years! Watch from the Kimberling City Bridge, a boat on the lake, or the shoreline. Over 1,000 boats gather every year. Dusk, roughly 9 PM. Free!

📍 Kimberling City Bridge • 🌇 Dusk (~9 PM) • FREE

Branson Landing Liberty Light Up

The 19th Annual — and they're promising "bigger than ever" for America's 250th. Live music: Cherry & James at 6 PM, The Rosy Hips at 8 PM. Choreographed fireworks with 120-foot fire cannons and water jets at ~9:20 PM. Free!

Fishing Report

Table Rock Lake & Lake Taneycomo — July 2, 2026

Best bet: Hit Table Rock at dawn for explosive topwater action, then work deep ledges 20–35 ft. On Taneycomo, wade the Trophy Run in the morning while generation is low.

Table Rock Lake
FishBiteBest Way
Largemouth / Spotted Bass🔥 HotTopwater at dawn on main-lake bluff points, then deep cranks/drop-shot 20–35 ft. 30–50 fish days!
Smallmouth Bass🔥 HotPBJ shaky head on deep ledges. Early morning topwater.
WalleyeGoodDeep rock 20–35 ft. Crawler harnesses, deep-diving crankbaits.
CrappieOKBrush piles 18–25 ft. Minnows or small jigs chartreuse/white.

🎯 Hot Tip from the Locals

Table Rock: With the heat index hitting 103°F, get on the water by 5:30–6 AM. The topwater bite fires early then shuts down fast — drop deep by 9 AM. Stay hydrated!

Taneycomo: Wade the Trophy Run in the morning — the water is a cool ~46–52°F. Heavy generation starts at 3 PM, making the river tough to fish on foot. Plan your morning around that window. Hot flies: Gray scuds, Ruby 2 midge, black copperhead midge.
