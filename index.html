🎣 Fishing Report

Table Rock & Lake Taneycomo — July 8, 2026

🎯 Bite of the Day

Morning topwater bass on Table Rock is heating up — hit points early with Whopper Ploppers before the sun gets high. On Taneycomo, the morning low-water window is your best bet; micro jigs in olive/gray under a float are producing 40+ fish days.

🏞️ Table Rock Lake

★★★★☆ Good

Surface temp: 77–85°F · Clarity: Clear to slight stain · Elevation: 916.88 ft

Best bet: Topwater bass at dawn (Whopper Ploppers, walking baits). As the sun climbs, fish deep with crankbaits and jigs on main lake points in 15–25 ft. Bluegill are excellent in shallows for kids — a July peak! Walleye good on deep points, crappie fair on brush piles in 15–25 ft.

🌊 Lake Taneycomo

★★★★☆ Good

Water temp: 48–52°F (released from deep Table Rock) · Clarity: Excellent, very clear

Best bet: Morning low-water window before generation ramps up. Micro jigs in olive, gray, or brown under a float. During generation (~12,800 cfs midday), drift seam edges and current breaks. A 24" / 6.12 lb rainbow caught recently. Plenty of 40+ fish days reported.

📊 Quick Summary

🐟 Table Rock — Topwater bass at dawn; deep jigs/crankbaits midday. Bluegill peak for kids.
🐟 Taneycomo — Morning low-water with micro jigs for trout. Drift seams during generation.
🎯 Bottom line: Early bird gets the worm (and the trout). Be on the water by 6 AM for best action.
