On the Water

🎣 Fishing Report

Get on Table Rock at dawn for topwater blow-ups, then grind deep ledges. Taneycomo is wadeable below the dam early — hit the Trophy Run with gray scuds before afternoon generation kicks in.

🎶 Table Rock Lake

MetricReading
Lake Level917.28 ft (~0.3 ft above summer pool)
Water Temp77.6°F (Kimberling City area)
ClarityClear on main lake; river arms stained
GenerationNear-zero overnight; ramps to 200 MW by afternoon

🐟 Bass (Largemouth/Spotted/Smallmouth) — Top Pick

Early AM: Topwater poppers, walking baits, buzzbaits on main-lake bluff points. PBJ shaky head deadly in Kings River.

Later: Move to 20–35 ft with deep crankbaits, drop-shot (green pumpkin/watermelon), shaky-head, Carolina rig.

Multiple charters reporting 30–50 fish days!

🐟 Walleye

Schooled on deep rock structure 20–35 ft. Crawler harnesses, deep-diving crankbaits in shad patterns. Nightcrawler rigs along ledges still producing limits.

🐟 Crappie & White Bass

Crappie in deep brush piles 18–25 ft — minnows or small chartreuse/white jigs. White bass chasing shad early AM — small spinners and Rooster Tails.

🐟 Bluegill

Swarming shallows — great for kids! Worms under a bobber near docks and brush. Simple, classic fun.

🎶 Lake Taneycomo

MetricReading
GenerationMinimal AM (wadeable!) → 200 MW (~13,000 cfs) 3–9 PM
Water Temp~46–52°F (cold tailwater — dress in layers!)
ClarityExcellent — clean presentations required
Best WindowBefore noon — heavy generation makes river unfishable on foot by afternoon

🎣 Below Dam / Trophy Area — Wading

Top flies: Gray scuds (#14–18), black copperhead midge (#16–18), Ruby 2 midge, olive wooly bugger. Provo Rig (double scud or mega worm + egg) is the go-to setup.

Hot colors: Red/pink San Juan worms, cerise San Juan worm, black copperhead midge.

Wade the Trophy Run early — move to deeper channels as sun climbs.

🚢 Boat / Drift Fishing

Early AM (low gen): Fish current seams with micro jigs under a float. Trout Magnet (salmon, 1/80 oz) at 4–6 ft. Big schools near MDC boat dock.

Drift rigs: Red/pink San Juan worms on light line (4 lb max moving, 2 lb no gen).

⚠️ Heavy generation alert: 200 MW (~13,000 cfs) from 3–9 PM. Fish early, be off the water by noon or prepare for rapidly changing conditions. Felt-soled boots prohibited on Taneycomo (MO regulation).
